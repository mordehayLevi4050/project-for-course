PRD: GrouPay — Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-groupay-2026-10-26/prd.md
Poor

The PRD is very precise for a project of this size. The settlement maths (FR-15, FR-19) gives one exact answer in every case, and all of its worked examples are correct when recomputed by hand. Scope and non-goals are stated explicitly, and the positioning is honest about Splid. The main risks are at the edges of the core journey rather than in its rigour. First, the PRD never specifies how to record an expense that someone other than the Organizer paid, even though the Vision says that is how half the trip's spending happens. Second, it treats converting foreign currency in people's heads on a trip abroad as a footnote (NFR-7), when that conversion directly distorts the accuracy and clarity metrics the trip is meant to test. Judged on the rubric alone, the PRD would be Fair: no dimension is broken and there are 2 high findings.

The grade drops to Poor because of two critical findings from the adversarial review. Both are small to fix in the text, but both would mislead architecture. (1) The "A → B ₪N" arrow does not mirror in a Hebrew RTL line, so the screenshot that the whole product exists to produce can show the payment going the wrong way. (2) The PRD defines the Transfer list as always being "the minimal set", yet FR-17/FR-19 require it to be saved and left unchanged while Paid marks exist. No stored entity is defined for it, and Paid marks point to Transfers only by the two names. The adversarial review also found eight high-severity gaps: no cap on Members while an exact minimum is required (an NP-hard calculation), no all-or-nothing saving for the multi-step "money was transferred" action, zero-Member division, and success metrics that can't be measured. The EARS consistency check found the uncommitted EARS rewrite largely aligned with the PRD. However, it adds founder-set Hebrew message texts and a lock message that the PRD does not contain, and the lock message contradicts the PRD's reason for the lock. Because the PRD says it wins wherever the two documents differ, those founder decisions are currently not protected.

Decision-readiness

adequate

The PRD makes real decisions and states their cost. §2.2 admits that v1 "deliberately does not solve" the original pain. §1 concedes that offline use and no signup are "table stakes, not an edge". §7 accepts the risk of data loss with no backup. What is missing is pushback on the one trade-off that most threatens the December test. §10 has a single Open Question, and it concerns later phases. §11 reports "No open assumptions", which is credible only if no live tension remains, and at least one does.

high

Currency conversion accepted without weighing its effect on the test

§5.2 NFR-7; §9 SM-2/3/4
The trip is in Rome, so almost every Expense is converted in someone's head. This weakens three metrics. SM-2: the 4-tap budget excludes the conversion step, which is now the slowest part of recording. SM-3: an amount can be disputed over the exchange rate even when FR-15 is perfect. SM-4: for the same reason. The PRD never weighs this against moving "Second currency with a fixed rate" ahead of the trip.
Fix: Add an Open Question that names the trade-off. At minimum, state a conversion convention (one agreed rate for the whole trip, plus a rounding rule).
medium

No open items despite a real-world launch

§10, §11
The founder's trip phone is never named, there is no Member cap, and recording other Members' payments after the fact is unspecified. All three are still open but are not listed.
Fix: Reopen the items that can't be settled before the build, or list them as accepted risks in §7.

Substance over theater

strong

Very little here is filler. There is one protagonist, Motty, and his traits feed directly into NFR-2 and FR-10. The Vision is specific to this product, and the NFRs carry product-specific numbers (4 taps, 48×48 dp, 2 s, 300 ms, tenths of a shekel).

low

"Look and feel" is a mood, not an input

§1
"Travel and financial confidence together" doesn't constrain any design choice.
Fix: Move it to the UX brief, or name concrete traits.

Strategic coherence

adequate

The thesis is clear: Hebrew-first, speed as the core design rule, short trips, and settling up with a screenshot. The counter-metrics are well chosen. The gap is that the thesis covers only the Organizer's own purchases. The path that SM-1 depends on most is recording Dana's fuel hours later, and it has no journey and no tap budget.

high

Recording an expense paid by another Member is unspecified

UJ-2, FR-10, NFR-1, SM-1
FR-10 defines only the default Payer. No FR says the Payer can be selected, how, or at what tap cost. There is also no way to back-date an expense, and the PRD does not say whether that is intended.
Fix: Add a journey (Motty records Dana's fuel that evening). Add a Payer-selection bullet with its own tap bound. State that the recorded time is when the expense was entered, not when it was paid.
medium

SM-1 and SM-5 have no measurement method or threshold

§9
SM-1 has nothing to check missing expenses against. SM-5's "minutes … not an evening" is not a measurable limit.
Fix: SM-1: after the trip, Members review the list and report misses, target 0. SM-5: ≤ 15 minutes.
low

SM-2 checks the spec, not the bet

§9 SM-2
SM-2 restates NFR-1, so it passes automatically if the app is built as specified.
Fix: Reframe it as an observed outcome, such as the median time from opening the app to a saved Expense.

Done-ness clarity

adequate

Most FRs are exemplary: messages are quoted, limits are given, and FR-15 is precise enough to write tests from directly. The weak spots fall on the screens and limits that the stories will be judged on.

medium

No Member cap, yet FR-15 requires an exact minimum plus a tie-break

FR-15, NFR-16
Finding the minimum set of transfers requires a combinatorial search. Tests cover only 2–6 Members, while FR-2 allows any number.
Fix: Cap group size (for example 12), or add a performance limit for the Summary.
medium

"Understandable from a screenshot alone" is not testable

FR-16
There is no acceptance procedure.
Fix: Name the content rules (Group name, Trip total, one "name → name ₪N" line per Transfer, paid state shown as text) and leave the human judgment to SM-4.
medium

Reference devices are unnamed

FR-16, NFR-5
"The founder's trip phone" and "a mid-range Android phone" can't be tested by anyone else.
Fix: Name the model or a dp viewport (for example 360×780 dp).
low

Lock explanation has no message text

FR-18
Every other refusal in the PRD quotes its message. This one doesn't.
Fix: Quote the message. See also EARS H1.
low

Default-Payer edge cases

FR-10
The default is undefined when the most recent Expense is deleted, or when the first Member added has been removed.
Fix: Use "the most recent remaining Expense, otherwise the earliest remaining Member".
low

Archive view entry point unspecified

FR-6
The PRD doesn't say where the Archive view is reached from or what it shows when empty.
Fix: Add one bullet covering both.

Scope honesty

strong

Omissions are stated at three levels: an Out-of-Scope line for each feature, the §6.2 deferrals, and the §7 Non-Goals. Accepted risks are stated plainly, and nothing looks silently dropped from scope. The only weakness, an open-items count of zero, is covered under Decision-readiness.

Downstream usability

strong

The Glossary is complete and used consistently. FR and SM IDs run in order without gaps, FRs link back to the user journeys they realize, and §8.1 maps FRs to stories cleanly.

medium

Story sizing is uneven

§8.1
Story 3 combines the hardest algorithm with the Paid-mark state machine. Story 1 spans 9 FRs.
Fix: Note an internal split of Story 3: calculation engine and tests, then Summary UI and Paid marks.
low

Some journey links point to journeys that don't show the action

FR-3, FR-5
No user journey renames a Member (FR-3) or a Group (FR-5).
Fix: Drop the links, or add the action to the journey.

Shape fit

strong

This is a consumer app with one user and meaningful UX, and the PRD feeds UX, architecture and stories. One protagonist with four journeys is the right amount. NFR-11–14 lean toward prescribing architecture, but §0 justifies them and they are kept narrow.

Adversarial review

review-adversarial-general.md

Critical 2 · High 8 · Medium 12 · Low 7. "The PRD is unusually precise about the arithmetic. That precision hides structural gaps around it. … The document is marked status: final with 'No open assumptions'. That claim is not credible. Fix the critical and high items before architecture starts."

critical

C1 · Transfer arrow reverses meaning in RTL

Glossary "Transfer", FR-15/16, NFR-6
The → character does not mirror in right-to-left text. "יוסי → דנה" is laid out with the arrow pointing at the debtor, so the screenshot says Dana pays Yossi. This is the product's single output.
Fix: Add an NFR that every direction must be unambiguous in RTL. Use direction-free wording ("יוסי משלם לדנה ₪100", meaning "Yossi pays Dana ₪100"), or use ← with each name isolated for bidi. Add a test on a rendered screenshot.
critical

C2 · Transfer list is defined as "the minimal set" but must be saved and frozen while locked

Glossary, FR-15, FR-17, FR-19
The list is either always recalculated (then the residual-equals-recomputed assumption is unproven, and Paid marks keyed by name pair can be left pointing at Transfers that no longer exist) or saved as a snapshot (then NFR-11 is missing that stored entity and its IDs). Architecture will choose one without anyone deciding.
Fix: Snapshot the list at the first Paid mark, give each Transfer a stable ID, and have marks reference that ID. Alternatively, prove the invariant as a property test.
high

H1 · No Member cap, but an exact minimum plus tie-break is required

FR-2, FR-15, NFR-5/16
Finding the minimum set of transfers is NP-hard. A 15-Member group could freeze the Summary.
Fix: Cap Members, add a latency NFR for the Summary, and extend the tests up to the cap.
high

H2 · Groups with zero or one Member

FR-10, FR-15, FR-4
With zero Members the Share divides by zero and there is no Payer to choose. The PRD doesn't say whether Add expense is enabled in that case.
Fix: Disable Add expense below the minimum, compute Share only when there are 2 or more Members, and add tests.
high

H3 · No all-or-nothing saving or double-tap protection

NFR-9, FR-19, FR-10
"Yes, transferred" clears the mark, records a Payment and may unlock the Group. If the app is killed partway, money is double-counted or lost. A double tap on Save creates a duplicate Expense.
Fix: Add an NFR that every action is saved all-or-nothing, and make commit actions ignore repeated taps.
high

H4 · The time-based tie-break depends on the phone's clock, and the save-order number isn't server-ready

FR-15, NFR-11/12
If the clock is wrong when an expense is recorded, its time is wrong even though it is stored in UTC. A per-device sequence number will collide across devices in the multi-user phase.
Fix: Make the sequence number or a logical clock the primary order, and keep the order source replaceable.
high

H5 · A "no disputes" goal with no way for friends to check amounts

§7 Non-Goals, FR-16, SM-3/4
The screenshot can't answer "why do I owe ₪1,250?".
Fix: Show the per-person Share on the Summary, or record disputes as an accepted risk with a counter-metric.
high

H6 · Success metrics are self-judged, have nothing to check against, and can't be read from the device

§9, §7, NFR-8/10
SM-1 has nothing to check against. The founder judges SM-4 and SM-5 on his own build. Reading the counter-metrics needs data that can't be taken off the phone.
Fix: Add a hidden stats export or a documented adb procedure, and give each metric a threshold.
high

H7 · The lock and archive rules cut across all three stories but are scheduled in Story 3

§8.1, FR-18, FR-6
Stories 1 and 2 ship without the guards, so Story 3 has to reopen them.
Fix: Define a single "can modify Group" check in Story 1, and split Story 3.
high

H8 · Names with no length limit break the one-screen Summary

FR-1, FR-2, FR-16
A 40-character name wraps every Transfer line.
Fix: Set maximum lengths (for example Member ≤ 20, Group ≤ 30), or specify how names are truncated.
medium

EARS ↔ PRD consistency

review-ears-consistency.md

Critical 0 · High 2 · Medium 5 · Low 14. Every FR from FR-1 to FR-19 has an EARS counterpart. All 40+ numeric test cases recompute correctly. The problems come from the uncommitted EARS rewrite, which adds founder-set message texts that the PRD does not contain. Since the PRD wins where the two differ, those decisions are not protected.

high

H1 · The EARS lock message contradicts the PRD's reason for the lock

EARS l.18, l.187 · PRD FR-18
EARS: "The group is locked to changes after transfers between members". PRD: the group is locked "because a payment was marked". A Paid mark can be set by mistake, so the EARS wording claims money moved when it may not have.
Fix: Quote the exact message in PRD FR-18, have the founder confirm the wording, and follow the Glossary ("a Transfer was marked as paid").
high

H2 · The founder's fixed Hebrew messages exist only in EARS

EARS l.11–18 · PRD NFR-6, §11
EARS introduces a binding requirement for exact Hebrew text, and the PRD has no counterpart.
Fix: Add the table to NFR-6, or name EARS as the source of truth for UI strings.
medium

M1 · The "Description required" message is missing from the PRD

EARS l.124 · PRD FR-11
FR-11 quotes a message for every other validation rule.
Fix: Add the message to FR-11, and state whether a whitespace-only Description counts as empty.
medium

M2 · Leftover tie rule incomplete in EARS

EARS l.164 · PRD FR-15
EARS is missing the rule for a receiver with no Expenses, a case reachable through Payments.
Fix: Add the rule and a test to EARS.
medium

M3 · FR-8 "set active at any time" has no EARS requirement

EARS l.32–36 · PRD FR-8
EARS covers only setting the active group from the creation prompt, and setting it while locked.
Fix: Add the requirement and a test to EARS.
medium

M4 · NFRs largely absent from EARS

PRD NFR-2–14, 17
EARS has no tests for NFR-5 (speed), NFR-6 (mixed Hebrew/Latin text in RTL), NFR-8 (offline) or NFR-9 (data survives restarts).
Fix: Add a cross-cutting section to EARS, or state in the PRD that EARS covers functional behavior only.
medium

M5 · Only 4 of about 13 messages have fixed Hebrew text

EARS l.13–18 · PRD NFR-6
Nothing says whether the remaining messages may be translated freely.
Fix: Complete the table, or state in both documents that only the 4 listed messages are fixed.
low

Mechanical notes

  • Stale boilerplate in §0: it mentions [ASSUMPTION] tags that don't exist, and a research section that isn't in the addendum.
  • Glossary capitalization drifts ("Archived group" / "archived Group"). "Group page", "Expense screen" and "Archive view" are not defined in the Glossary.
  • NFR-1 never states that tap counting starts after the launcher icon tap.
  • Cross-references all resolve. The Assumptions Index is consistent: zero tags and zero entries.
